# Hardware Return and Damage Policy

## Standard Hardware Damage
If a Standard Tier user reports hardware damage (e.g., shattered laptop screens, dented chassis, or malfunctioning keyboards):
1. **Photo Evidence Required:** The support agent MUST request that the customer reply to the email thread with photo evidence of the damage. No refunds or replacements can be processed without photographic proof.
2. **Standard SLA:** Once photos are received, the agent must create a Medium Severity support ticket. A human agent will review the photos within 48 business hours.

## VIP Hardware Damage
VIP users receive expedited, white-glove resolution for hardware damage.
1. **Immediate Replacement / Refund Initiation:** Photo evidence is NOT strictly required to begin the refund or replacement process for VIP users, though it is encouraged.
2. **Mandatory Manager Escalation:** For any VIP reporting severe hardware damage (like a shattered screen), the agent MUST use the calendar booking tool to schedule a 15-minute follow-up mediation call with a Customer Success Manager for the next available morning slot.
3. **VIP SLA:** The agent must immediately create a High Severity support ticket.

## General Exclusions
We do not offer refunds for hardware damage caused by liquid spills. If a customer mentions water, coffee, or liquid damage, inform them that this voids the standard warranty and create a Low Severity ticket for the repair team to quote a repair price.
