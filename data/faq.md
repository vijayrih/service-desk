# General Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## How do I get a copy of my invoice?
Users can download all past invoices and billing receipts directly from their Customer Portal under the "Billing History" tab. The agent should guide them there. Do not create a support ticket for invoice requests.

## How do I upgrade my account to VIP status?
VIP status is handled by the Enterprise Sales Team and requires a custom contract. 
*   **Action:** If a Standard user asks to become VIP, create a LOW Severity support ticket categorized as 'Sales Inquiry' and inform the user that a sales representative will email them shortly.

## What are your human support hours?
While the Autonomous Service Desk is available 24/7, human IT and Customer Success Managers are only available for calendar bookings Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM (Eastern Standard Time). 

## Can I change the email address associated with my account?
For security reasons, email addresses cannot be changed manually by the user. 
*   **Action:** Create a MEDIUM Severity ticket categorized as 'Account Management' and inform the user that a security specialist will reach out to verify their identity before making the change.
